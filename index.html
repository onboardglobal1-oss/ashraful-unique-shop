Cyber Security Awareness

Online Fraud Warning
  • Never pay “advance fees” for lottery, prize or job offers.
  • Scanning a QR code is only for paying. You never scan to receive money.
  • Confirm any payment request by calling the person on a number you already know.
  • Report fraud quickly on 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.
Account Security
  • Use a different password of 12+ characters for every account.
  • Turn on two-step verification (2FA) everywhere.
  • Check “logged-in devices” regularly and remove unknown ones.
  • Use a trusted password manager instead of notes or screenshots.
Phishing Awareness
  • Check the real sender address and the link spelling before you click.
  • Be careful with urgent messages like “account blocked” or “KYC expired”.
  • Never install APK files sent on WhatsApp, SMS or Telegram.
  • Banks never ask for your OTP, PIN or CVV.
Social Media Safety
  • Keep your profile private and accept only people you know.
  • Don’t post your phone number, location or documents publicly.
  • Ignore fake giveaways, “copyright strike” and “account recovery” DMs.
  • Report and block suspicious accounts.
Privacy Protection
  • Install apps only from the Play Store or App Store.
  • Remove camera, mic and contacts permission that an app doesn’t need.
  • Keep your phone and apps updated, and use a screen lock.
  • Avoid banking on public Wi-Fi.
CYBER WARNING: Don’t share your OTP, password or personal details with anyone.

Ethical Hacking

Learn how security experts legally find weaknesses so they can be fixed before criminals misuse them.

Ethical hacking means testing a website, app or network with the owner’s permission to find and fix security problems. People who do this legally are called white-hat hackers.

White Hat vs Black Hat
  • White hat: works with the owner’s permission, stays legal and reports weaknesses to get them fixed.
  • Black hat: breaks in without permission to steal, damage or cheat. This is a crime.
  • Grey hat: no bad intention, but testing without permission is still not allowed.
Learning Roadmap
  • Computer and networking basics (IP, DNS, HTTP).
  • Linux and Windows basics.
  • Python and basic web (HTML, JavaScript).
  • Security fundamentals: encryption, passwords and the OWASP Top 10.
  • Practice only in legal labs.
Legal Practice Platforms
  • TryHackMe and Hack The Box for guided labs.
  • PortSwigger Web Security Academy for web security.
  • OWASP Juice Shop, a safe app made to practise on.
Careers & Certifications
  • Security analyst, penetration tester and bug bounty researcher.
  • Certifications: CompTIA Security+, CEH, eJPT and OSCP.
  • Many companies run bug bounty programs and reward responsible reports.
Rules to Follow
  • Always take written permission before testing anything.
  • Never touch other people’s accounts, websites or networks.
  • Report a weakness responsibly and don’t misuse data.
  • In India, unauthorised access is punishable under the IT Act, 2000.

Cyber Safety

  • Never share OTP, PIN, CVV or passwords with anyone.
  • Think before you click. Check links and sender first.
  • Use 2-step verification on email, WhatsApp, Instagram and banking apps.
  • Keep software and apps updated.
  • Don’t trust unknown calls claiming to be police, bank or courier.
  • Back up important photos and files regularly.

Became a victim of fraud? Do this now

  1. Call the National Cyber Crime Helpline  1930  immediately.
  2. File a complaint on cybercrime.gov.in.
  3. Inform your bank and block the card or UPI.
  4. Change passwords and log out of all devices.
  5. Keep screenshots, numbers and transaction IDs as proof.

FAQ

Common Questions

My social media account was hacked. What should I do?

Use the app’s official “Forgot password” or recovery option, log out of all devices, change your password, turn on 2FA and warn your friends not to trust messages from your account.

Is it safe to share an OTP with customer care?

No. Banks and companies never ask for your OTP. Anyone who asks is likely a scammer.

How do I know a link is fake?

Check the spelling of the domain, avoid shortened links from strangers and open banking or shopping sites only from the official app or website.
